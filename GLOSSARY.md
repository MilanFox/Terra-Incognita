# Terra Incognita

Geography quiz games played on a world map.

## Language

**Mode**:
One kind of game with its own rules, such as find on the map or fill the map.
_Avoid_: Game type, quiz type

**Tier**:
One of three cumulative sets of askable places: sovereign, de facto, dependencies. Each tier contains the one before it.
_Avoid_: Difficulty, level

**Sovereign tier**:
The 197 widely recognised sovereign states: the 193 UN members plus Vatican City, Palestine, Kosovo and Taiwan.

**De facto tier**:
The sovereign tier plus states that govern themselves without wide recognition, such as Somaliland.

**Dependencies tier**:
The de facto tier plus territories governed by another state.

**De facto borders**:
Borders drawn by who actually controls the land, not by who claims it. The single stance used for every tier.

**Microstate**:
A place whose largest piece of land is too small to spot at whole-world view.
_Avoid_: Tiny country

**Unclaimed land**:
Land on the map that belongs to no place and is never asked, such as Antarctica or Bir Tawil.
_Avoid_: No man's land, terra nullius

**Region**:
One of six parts of the world: Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, South America, Oceania. Every place belongs to exactly one.
_Avoid_: Continent

**Region filter**:
A restriction of a round to one region, such as only Africa. A round is either whole world or one region.

**Place**:
Anything a round can ask about: a sovereign state, a de facto state or a dependency.
_Avoid_: Country, entity, territory

**Pool**:
The places a round can ask about: the tier, narrowed by any region filter and by what the mode can ask.

**Round**:
One playthrough of a mode, from setup to results.
_Avoid_: Game, session, run

**Deck**:
The shuffled prompts of a round, each place asked at most once.

**Prompt**:
One question within a round, about one place.
_Avoid_: Question, card

**Variant**:
The form a mode's prompts take for a whole round, such as by flag or by silhouette.

**Easy mode**:
A per-round switch that makes answering more forgiving, with extra attempts or multiple choice instead of typing.
_Avoid_: Difficulty, level

**Timed round**:
A round with one countdown for the whole round; when it runs out the round ends as if ended early. Untimed rounds show no clock.

**End round**:
Stopping a round before its deck is empty; prompts not yet reached count as missed.
_Avoid_: Give up, quit

**Daily challenge**:
One round per calendar day that is the same for every player.

**Streak**:
The count of consecutive days on which the daily challenge was completed.
